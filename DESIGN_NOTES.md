# Ratatune V3 — Design Notes

## Core idea

The landing page behaves like a product demonstration. The visitor should not feel that they are reading a marketing brochure; each section reveals one more piece of the same question:

> Where did my bend actually land?

## First 10 seconds

- Persistent glass navigation establishes a calm premium frame.
- The exact campaign promise appears immediately.
- A supplied Ratatune/harmonica visual provides product specificity rather than generic AI imagery.
- Floating target/actual/cents readings create a reason to inspect the visual.
- The CTA is visible immediately and remains singular: **Get Early Access**.
- Small motion in the background, orbital linework and readouts adds life without turning the hero into a noisy animation demo.

## Scroll psychology, without dark patterns

The page uses progressive disclosure and visual curiosity rather than fake scarcity or forced interactions:

- The problem creates an unanswered question.
- The signal section shows that the answer can be measured.
- The Bend Lab makes the measurement process unfold over scroll.
- The instrument section grounds the experience in real harmonica technique.
- The proof section rewards continued scrolling with the supplied product reading.
- The final section resolves the story with honest pre-launch access.

## Bend Lab

The pinned sequence is the centerpiece:

**PLAY → MEASURE → ADJUST → REPEAT**

Each state crossfades with blur/translation while the phone, orbiting rings, scan light and progress bar move continuously. The animation is scrubbed by scroll so forward and reverse scrolling remain natural.

## Visual language

- Deep forest/charcoal: technical depth and focus.
- Warm gold: measurement, signal and action.
- Warm paper: editorial contrast and breathing room.
- Serif display typography: musical/editorial character.
- Compact uppercase metadata: instrument/product-system feel.
- Rounded surfaces are used selectively; the page avoids card-grid overload.

## Proof discipline

The −28 cents reading is presented as the supplied product evidence, not as a fabricated performance claim. The page does not invent pricing, live availability, user counts, or production maturity.
