# Ratatune V2 — Senior Frontend Build Contract

## Objective
Create a premium, product-specific pre-launch landing page for Ratatune's real-time harmonica bend accuracy feature. The client supplied a RingUs website as a direction reference; use the reference for experience ambition, scroll rhythm and visual confidence, not for copying its identity.

## Non-negotiables from the assignment
- Core promise: “Finally hear whether your bends are actually in tune.”
- Audience: intermediate → advanced harmonica players.
- Product: AI-powered app that listens through a microphone and gives real-time bend feedback.
- Proof must be specific: target, actual pitch, cents, and/or a concrete adjustment.
- Use harmonica vocabulary: bends, draw notes, embouchure, breath, cents, semitones.
- Product is pre-launch. Do not imply a finished public app.
- CTA is only “Get Early Access”.
- Lead capture: required email + one optional qualifying field.
- No price claim.
- No generic stock/template/SaaS treatment.

## Experience direction
The first 8–10 seconds should communicate the product without requiring a paragraph. The hero combines the supplied harmonica/player/product visual with the exact promise and a visible −28 cents reading. The scroll then becomes a product story:

1. Promise / hero
2. Why bends are difficult to judge
3. Sound → number visualization
4. Pinned PLAY → MEASURE → ADJUST → REPEAT scene
5. Bend-specific feature language
6. Harmonica craft / context
7. Real product proof
8. Pre-launch honesty
9. One final early-access CTA

## Motion system
- Lenis for smooth scrolling.
- GSAP ScrollTrigger for scroll choreography and the pinned Bend Lab.
- Motion for React for modal transitions.
- Keep movement purposeful: no gratuitous floating UI.
- Support prefers-reduced-motion.
- Scroll backward naturally; all scroll-linked states must be reversible.

## Visual system
- Forest / charcoal for audio, practice and product moments.
- Warm paper for editorial explanation and proof.
- Gold reserved for CTA and measurement/signal moments.
- Serif display typography for human craft; sans for interface/readouts.
- Large responsive type using clamp().
- Strong whitespace and asymmetrical editorial grids.
- Avoid a generic three-card SaaS hero.

## Asset policy
Use the supplied product and harmonica imagery as evidence, not decoration. The final concept retains the supplied campaign/reference imagery inside an editorial reference strip so the source material is visibly part of the art direction.

## Quality bar
The result should feel deliberately art-directed by a senior product designer/frontend engineer: product-specific, restrained, cinematic, responsive, accessible, and honest. Do not invent performance statistics or user outcomes.
